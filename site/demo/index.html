Can you beat it?

We broke one part of a real online shop on purpose, 14 times, and crash-tested our own RCA agent on each. Guess which service broke, then watch it investigate. Notice when it says verified, and when it refuses to. Recorded runs, misses included.

The shop is Online Boutique: eleven services in Docker, lines showing who calls whom. Faults were drawn at random and written down before each round. Round 1 measured latency only inside each service; rounds 2 and 3 also measured it from the callers. The AI decision model is TypeSafe's Jev; plain code decides when an answer counts as verified.
