A better score doesn't mean a safer agent.

  • Right answer, wrong reasons.

    A higher accuracy number doesn't tell you whether the agent ignored evidence against its answer, or stopped before checking the alternatives.

  • Self-checks aren't proof.

    The team that builds an agent shouldn't be the only one deciding whether it worked. Neither should the agent.

  • Human approval doesn't scale.

    If an engineer has to re-check every recommendation by hand, the agent hasn't earned autonomy. It has moved the work.

Step 2, shadow mode, comes next. TypeRCA runs read-only next to your existing agent on your real incidents and logs a verdict for each recommendation: VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED or CONTRADICTED. Nothing is blocked or automated. Afterwards the verdicts are checked against what really happened, so you see which recommendations your agent has earned the right to act on.
